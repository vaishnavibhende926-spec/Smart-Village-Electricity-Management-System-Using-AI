Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do during a power outage?

First check whether the outage affects only your house or the surrounding area. You can report the outage through the complaint form above.

What if my electricity meter shows abnormal readings?

Select "Meter Problem" and provide the observed reading or issue in the complaint description.

Can I report a billing problem?

Yes. Select "Billing Issue" and describe the difference or problem you observed.

How will my complaint be handled?

Your complaint is stored in the system. An authorized electricity officer can review it and update its status.
